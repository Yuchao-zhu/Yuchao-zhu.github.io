Xinyue is a PhD student working in the field of molecular biology. She completed her master's training at the Tianjin Institute of Industrial Biotechnology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, where she developed strong skills in base editing and high-throughput screening. Building on that foundation, she is now applying these techniques to identify novel GPCR ligands and explore new possibilities in receptor pharmacology.
